You are taking part in a blind image-evaluation study.

You will receive 6 contact sheets with 150 small colour images, numbered 1 to 150. Each image is 32x32 pixels (shown enlarged) and comes from the CIFAR-10 domain: airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck. Some images are real photographs from CIFAR-10 and some were produced by image-generation models. You are not told how many of each there are, and the proportions are not necessarily equal, so judge every image on its own.

For EVERY image, give:
1. realism: an integer from 1 to 5
   1 = clearly artificial (heavy blur, smeared shapes, no coherent object)
   2 = mostly artificial, a few plausible elements
   3 = uncertain or mixed
   4 = mostly realistic, minor oddities
   5 = indistinguishable from a real low-resolution photograph
2. class: the CIFAR-10 class the image shows, using exactly one of: airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck. If you cannot recognise any of these objects, write: unrecognisable
3. source: "real" if you believe it is a real photograph, otherwise "generated"

Rules:
- Judge the images independently and only from what you see. Do not try to balance your answers across classes or sources.
- Low resolution alone is not a sign of being generated: every real image is also only 32x32 pixels.
- Rate all 150 images. Do not skip any and do not add explanations.

Output format: plain CSV only, with this header line and then exactly one line per image, in order from 1 to 150:
id,realism,class,source
1,4,horse,real
2,1,unrecognisable,generated
...
